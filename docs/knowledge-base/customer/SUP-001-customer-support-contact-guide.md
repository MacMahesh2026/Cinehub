## 1. Before you contact us

Many issues can be resolved faster by checking the FAQ first. For booking problems, please have your booking reference ready. You can find it on the confirmation page, on the booking details page, and in My Bookings.

Please contact us from the email address registered on your CineHub account where possible. This helps us verify that we are speaking with the account holder.

## 2. Contact channels

| Channel | Use it for | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email support | Account help, booking problems, cancellation help, general questions | support@cinehub.example |
| Privacy email | Data access, correction, deletion, privacy complaints | privacy@cinehub.example |
| Phone | Urgent booking or account help during support hours | +91 00000 00000 |
| Help Centre | Policies, FAQs, and downloadable guides | /help |
| Security contact | Suspected vulnerability or account compromise | security@cinehub.example |

Support hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00 IST, excluding public holidays. Messages received outside these hours are queued for the next business day.

CineHub does not currently provide live chat or a WhatsApp support channel. Do not rely on unofficial accounts claiming to represent CineHub.

## 3. What to include in your message

To help us investigate quickly, include:

- your full name;
- the email address registered on your CineHub account;
- your booking reference, if the issue is about a booking;
- the movie title, theatre, screen, showtime, and seat numbers, where relevant;
- a clear description of what happened;
- the exact error message you saw, if any;
- the date and time of the issue; and
- the browser and device you used, if the problem is technical.

Do not send your password, card number, bank details, UPI PIN, or one-time passwords. CineHub support does not need them and will never ask for them.

## 4. Response times

Our target response times are:

| Request type | First response target | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General question | Within 2 business days | When answered |
| Booking or cancellation issue | Within 1 business day | Within 3 business days where possible |
| Account access issue | Within 1 business day | Depends on identity verification |
| Privacy request | Within 30 days, or sooner where required by law | Depends on the request |
| Suspected security issue | Within 1 business day | Based on severity |

These are targets, not guarantees. Complex issues, legal requests, or cases requiring theatre involvement may take longer. We will keep you informed if an issue takes longer than expected.

## 5. Common issues and the fastest route

### Registration is closed

CineHub currently allows up to 10 registered accounts. When the limit is reached, new registration is closed and the form shows: "System allows up to 10 users only. Registration is closed."

There is no waiting list. Contact support if you believe you need access. Do not create duplicate accounts.

### You already have 3 confirmed bookings

CineHub allows a maximum of 3 confirmed bookings per user. If you see: "Maximum 3 bookings are allowed per user", cancel one of your confirmed bookings from My Bookings, then try again.

### You cannot cancel a booking

Cancellation is available only for bookings with the status CONFIRMED. If a booking is already cancelled, the system returns: "Only confirmed bookings can be cancelled." If the cancel button is not working, confirm that you are signed in to the account that made the booking.

### A seat is already booked

CineHub does not hold seats temporarily. Another user may have booked the seat before you submitted. Choose a different seat and try again.

### You did not receive a confirmation email

CineHub does not currently send booking confirmation emails or SMS messages. Your confirmation is shown on screen and your booking appears in My Bookings. Save your booking reference.

### You forgot your password

CineHub does not currently provide a self-service password reset. Contact support for help. Support may ask you to verify your identity before assisting with account access.

### You want to delete your account

Account deletion is not currently self-service. Email privacy@cinehub.example from the registered address and follow the verification steps you are given.

## 6. Escalation

If your issue is not resolved within the target time, reply to the same email thread and ask for escalation. Please include the original request and any reference number you were given.

Escalation path:

1. Customer support agent.
2. Support lead.
3. Product or operations owner for system issues.
4. Privacy contact for data protection complaints.
5. Security contact for suspected vulnerabilities or account compromise.

We will explain the next step and expected timeline when an issue is escalated.

## 7. Privacy and data requests

Send privacy requests to privacy@cinehub.example. Include:

- your full name;
- the email address on your account;
- the right you want to exercise (access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection); and
- any details that will help us locate the relevant data.

We may ask for identity verification. We aim to respond within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law. See the Privacy, Cookies and Data Retention Policy for more information.

## 8. Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, email security@cinehub.example with:

- a description of the issue;
- steps to reproduce it;
- the potential impact; and
- any supporting screenshots or logs that do not contain another person's personal data.

Please do not test the issue against another user's account, disrupt the service, or publicly disclose the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password if you can, log out of other sessions where possible, and contact support immediately.

## 9. What support cannot do

CineHub support cannot currently:

- create an account after the 10-account limit is full without an approved capacity decision;
- allow more than 3 confirmed bookings on one account;
- process online payments or online refunds;
- send booking confirmation emails or SMS messages;
- restore a cancelled booking;
- provide self-service password reset; or
- guarantee entry to a theatre or override theatre rules.

Support will explain the available options rather than promise a feature that the system does not provide.
