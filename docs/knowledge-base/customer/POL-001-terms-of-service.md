## 4. Movies, showtimes, and seat availability

- Movie details, theatre details, showtimes, seat maps, and prices are supplied as data in the CineHub system.
- Showtimes are displayed as they are stored in the system. The service does not currently filter out showtimes that have already passed.
- Seat availability is checked when you submit a booking. CineHub does not place a temporary hold on seats while you are selecting them.
- A seat is not reserved for you until a booking has been successfully created and marked as confirmed.
- The price shown for a showtime applies to the seats in that booking. CineHub does not currently apply different prices by seat type.

## 5. Bookings and confirmation

- To create a booking, select an available showtime, choose at least one seat, and submit the booking form.
- A successful booking is created with the status CONFIRMED.
- Each booking receives a booking reference in the format CH- followed by 10 letters or numbers.
- Each user may hold a maximum of 3 confirmed bookings at a time. Cancelled bookings do not count toward this limit.
- The booking confirmation is displayed on screen. CineHub does not currently send confirmation emails or SMS messages.
- You should save your booking reference and keep it available when contacting support or attending the theatre.
- You can view your bookings in the My Bookings area of the service.

## 6. Payments and refunds

- CineHub does not currently provide an online payment gateway and does not collect card, bank, or wallet details.
- A booking is marked CONFIRMED without an online payment step.
- The amount shown on the booking is the showtime price multiplied by the number of selected seats. CineHub does not currently calculate separate taxes, convenience fees, or seat-type surcharges.
- Because no online payment is collected by CineHub, CineHub does not process online refunds.
- Any payment, refund, or adjustment handled directly by a theatre is a matter between you and that theatre and is outside the CineHub application.

## 7. Cancellation

- You can cancel a booking only while its status is CONFIRMED.
- Cancellation is available from the booking details page and requires the booking to belong to your account.
- CineHub does not currently apply a cancellation deadline. A confirmed booking can be cancelled even after the showtime has passed.
- Cancellation is immediate and cannot be undone. The booking status changes to CANCELLED, the seats are released for other users, and the cancelled booking no longer counts toward your 3-booking limit.
- Cancelled bookings remain visible in your booking history.
- No online refund is issued because CineHub does not collect online payments.

- contacting support promptly if you believe your account or a booking has been misused.
