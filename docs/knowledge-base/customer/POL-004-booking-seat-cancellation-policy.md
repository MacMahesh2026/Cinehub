## 1. Scope

This policy explains how to book a movie ticket on CineHub, how seats are allocated, the current booking limits, and how cancellation works.

It should be read together with our Terms of Service, Account and Registration Policy, and Privacy, Cookies and Data Retention Policy.

## 2. Browsing movies and showtimes

- The home page lists the movies stored in the CineHub catalogue.
- The movie details page shows the movie information and the showtimes stored for that movie.
- Showtimes are displayed as they are stored in the system. CineHub does not currently hide showtimes whose start time has already passed.
- CineHub does not currently provide search, genre filters, city filters, or date filters.
- Prices are set per showtime and are shown in Indian rupees (INR).

## 3. Seat selection

- The seat page shows the seats configured for the selected screen.
- Seats that are already booked for the showtime are shown as unavailable and cannot be selected.
- CineHub does not place a temporary hold on a seat while you are choosing. A seat is only reserved when a booking is successfully created.
- If you submit without selecting a seat, CineHub returns: "Please select at least one seat."
- If a selected seat is already booked, CineHub returns a message such as: "Seat A1 is already booked."
- If a seat does not belong to the selected screen, the booking is rejected as an invalid seat.
- The price shown for the showtime applies to each selected seat. CineHub stores a seat type but does not currently charge different prices by seat type.

## 4. Booking limit: 3 confirmed bookings per user

Each CineHub account may hold a maximum of 3 confirmed bookings at the same time.

- The limit is checked when you submit a booking.
- Only bookings with the status CONFIRMED count toward the limit.
- If you already have 3 confirmed bookings, CineHub returns: "Maximum 3 bookings are allowed per user."
- To make another booking, cancel one of your confirmed bookings first. Cancelling frees a slot because cancelled bookings do not count toward the limit.
- The limit applies per account. Creating multiple accounts to bypass the limit is prohibited.
- The service also has a separate limit of 10 registered accounts in total. See the Account and Registration Policy.

## 5. Creating a booking

1. Sign in to your CineHub account.
2. Choose a movie and a showtime.
3. Select one or more available seats.
4. Confirm the booking.
5. CineHub creates the booking with the status CONFIRMED and shows a confirmation page.

A booking reference is generated in the format CH- followed by 10 letters or numbers, for example CH-1A2B3C4D5E. The reference is displayed on the confirmation page, on the booking details page, and in My Bookings.

The confirmation is shown only on screen. CineHub does not currently send a confirmation email, SMS, or push notification. Save your booking reference and take a screenshot if needed.

## 6. Price and total amount

- The total amount is calculated as the showtime price multiplied by the number of selected seats.
- The amount is displayed in INR.
- CineHub does not currently add taxes, convenience fees, or seat-type surcharges.
- CineHub does not currently collect an online payment. The booking becomes CONFIRMED without a payment step.
- Any payment made directly to a theatre is separate from the CineHub application.

## 7. Cancelling a booking

You can cancel a booking from the booking details page.

- Cancellation is allowed only while the booking status is CONFIRMED.
- If the booking is already cancelled, CineHub returns: "Only confirmed bookings can be cancelled."
- CineHub does not currently apply a cancellation deadline. A confirmed booking may be cancelled even after the showtime has passed.
- A valid cancellation changes the booking status to CANCELLED.
- The seats are released and can be booked by another user.
- The cancelled booking no longer counts toward the 3-confirmed-booking limit.
- The cancelled booking remains visible in your booking history.
- Cancellation is final. CineHub does not currently provide a way to reopen or restore a cancelled booking.
- You cannot cancel a booking that belongs to another account.

## 8. Refunds

- CineHub does not currently collect online payments, so it does not process online refunds.
- Cancelling a booking changes the booking status and releases the seats. It does not create a payment, credit note, or refund transaction in the CineHub system.
- If you paid a theatre directly, ask that theatre about its refund and cancellation rules.
- Do not send card or bank details to CineHub support. CineHub does not need them to cancel a booking.

## 9. No-shows, late entry, and theatre rules

CineHub records the booking but does not control entry to the theatre. Theatre rules on late entry, age restrictions, food, bags, accessibility, and conduct apply in addition to this policy. Contact the theatre directly for venue-specific questions.

## 10. Booking problems

If a booking does not appear in My Bookings:

- confirm that you are signed in to the same account used to book;
- check that the booking reference is correct;
- refresh the page and try again; and
- contact support with your name, email address, booking reference, movie, theatre, screen, showtime, and selected seats.

If you see a seat as unavailable, another user may have booked it. CineHub does not maintain a waitlist or a temporary seat hold.
